QUY UOC VIET BAO CAO TTCS - FANG AI CORE

Muc dich
File nay ghi lai cach viet noi dung bao cao trong cac chat tiep theo. Muc tieu la tao noi dung co the copy sang DOCX de bien tap tiep.

1. Cach goi ban viet

Khong goi la "nhap" theo nghia viet tam hoac viet so sai.

Workflow dung:
1. Viet ban hoan chinh lan 1 cho tung chuong hoac tung cum muc.
2. Nguoi dung review ve giong van, do dai, muc chi tiet, cach dan evidence va cach goi FANG/miCareer-mini.
3. Ghi nhan nhan xet do nhu style guide cho chuong sau.
4. Sua chuong hien tai neu can, roi moi viet chuong tiep theo.

2. Dinh dang dau ra

Khi viet noi dung bao cao, uu tien tao file .txt hoac tra plain text.
Khong viet noi dung chuong bang Markdown vi nguoi dung se copy sang DOCX.

Duoc dung tieu de danh so kieu:
CHUONG 1. PHAM VI DU AN VA KIEN TRUC FANG AI CORE
1.1. Pham vi du an
1.1.1. Vai tro cua FANG

Khong dung Markdown heading (#, ##) trong ban noi dung chuong.
Khong dung bullet qua nhieu trong phan van xuoi chinh, tru khi do la danh sach can dua vao bao cao.

3. Cach danh dau bang, hinh, so do, evidence

Vi DOCX can chen bang/hinh rieng, moi doi tuong khong phai van xuoi phai duoc danh dau ro:

[BANG - Ten bang]
Mo ta bang hoac noi dung bang. Neu bang co cot/hang, ghi ro cot nao.

[HINH - Ten hinh]
Mo ta anh can chen, ngu canh, man hinh demo nao, muc dich cua anh.

[SO DO - Ten so do]
Mo ta so do bang text hoac Mermaid neu can. Neu dung AI image model, viet prompt mo ta ro.

[GHI CHU EVIDENCE]
Ghi file/tai lieu/code/test dung lam can cu. Phan nay co the giu trong ban soan thao va loc bot khi dua vao DOCX.

4. Yeu cau ve van phong

Van phong hoc thuat, ro rang, co he thong.
Khong marketing qua da.
Khong viet chung chung neu co the dan code/tai lieu/test.
Giai thich y nghia nghiep vu truoc, cong thuc/thuat toan sau, code reference cuoi.

5. Cac dinh vi bat buoc

FANG la san pham chinh: backend/AI Core.
miCareer-mini la frontend mong/dev-test UI de chung minh tich hop API, khong phai san pham trung tam.
FANG ho tro HR va candidate workflow, khong thay the quyet dinh tuyen dung cua HR.

Ranking phai tach ro:
- J->C: HR tim ung vien cho JobPosting.
- C->J: Candidate tim job phu hop truoc khi apply.

Chat/Agent phai tach ro:
- JobApplication Chat co scope jobAppId.
- JobPosting Agent co scope jobPostId.

6. Caveat bat buoc

Khong claim ranking chinh xac tuyet doi.
Khong claim FANG san sang production neu chua co evidence.
Neu nhac tuning/gain, phai ghi ro do la synthetic/LLM-judged benchmark.
Full-CV chat phu thuoc parse quality, prompt quality va token budget.
JobPosting Agent co guardrails/tool-calling/scope validation/PII masking, nhung van can eval va hardening neu dua len production.

7. Thu tu viet khuyen nghi

1. Viet ban hoan chinh lan 1 cho Chuong 1.
2. Nguoi dung review va chot style.
3. Viet Chuong 2 theo nhieu cum: ranking, synthetic/tuning, chat, agent.
4. Viet Chuong 3 ve tai lieu va kiem thu.
5. Viet Chuong 4 ve kien truc ma nguon.
6. Viet Chuong 5 ve demo, danh gia, han che, huong phat trien.
7. Viet phan mo dau va ket luan sau cung.

8. Kinh nghiem ve hinh va so do

Voi cac hinh/so do render tu Mermaid, moi dong hien thi trong node hoac message khong nen vuot qua khoang 26 ky tu/chu. Tuy nhien khong dung <br/> trong Mermaid vi VS Code Preview/DOCX export co the hien <br/> nhu text tho.

Quy uoc Mermaid moi:
- Flowchart: xuong dong label bang \n trong dau ngoac kep, vi du `A["Dong 1\nDong 2"]`.
- SequenceDiagram: khong chen <br/> va khong co xuong dong that trong message; neu message qua dai thi tach thanh nhieu message ngan hoac rut gon text.
- Trong label Mermaid, tranh viet `J->C` hoac `C->J` vi co the gay nham voi arrow syntax; dung `J→C`, `C→J`, hoac dien giai bang chu.
